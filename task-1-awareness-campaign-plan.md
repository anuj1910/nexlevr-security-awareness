# Task 1: 4-Week Cyber & AI Security Awareness Campaign Plan

**Track:** Cyber & AI Security · Security Awareness (Intermediate Track)  
**Intern:** Anuj Agrawal  
**Organization:** NEXLEVR  

---

## 1. Campaign Overview & Brand Focus
* **Campaign Theme / Brand Focus:** *AI Safe & Smart* — Everyday Cyber & AI Security Habits for Students, Creators, and Professionals
* **Campaign Duration:** 4 Weeks
* **Primary Objective:** Raise practical awareness around AI-assisted scams, prompt data privacy, and everyday account security, encouraging audiences to adopt simple, actionable verification and hygiene habits.
* **Key Message:** *"Use AI smartly—keep sensitive data out of public prompts and verify before you trust."*

---

## 2. Target Audience
* **Primary Audience:** College students, early-career professionals, and content creators who use generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, image/video generators) and social platforms daily.
* **Pain Points Addressed:**
  * Pasting confidential notes, credentials, or personal data into public AI tools.
  * Difficulty distinguishing AI-generated phishing messages, cloned voices, and deepfakes from legitimate outreach.
  * Password reuse and weak account recovery settings.

---

## 3. Distribution Channels
* **LinkedIn (Primary Reach & Discussion):** Educational carousels, concise text breakdowns, and interactive polls aimed at students and professionals.
* **GitHub (Documentation & Resource Hub):** Open repository hosting campaign plans, weekly checklists, visual assets, and campaign performance reports.

---

## 4. 4-Week Content Plan

| Week | Theme | Core Lesson / Topic | LinkedIn Format | GitHub Artifact | Interactive Hook / CTA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | **Prompt Privacy & Data Leaks** | What never to paste into public AI tools (API keys, passwords, IDs, private company/academic data) and how to sanitize prompts. | 5-slide visual carousel + summary post | `week-1-prompt-privacy.md` + checklist | LinkedIn Poll: *"Have you ever pasted work or school notes into an AI tool without removing names/details?"* |
| **Week 2** | **Spot the AI Scam** | Recognizing AI-polished phishing emails, deepfake profiles, and urgent voice/text impersonation scams. | Side-by-side "Real vs. AI Red Flags" visual post | `week-2-ai-phishing-redflags.md` | Mini-quiz in comments asking readers to spot 3 red flags in a sample message. |
| **Week 3** | **Account & Identity Hygiene** | Turning on phishing-resistant MFA, using password managers, and auditing third-party app permissions. | Short infographic + 3-step quick-action guide | `week-3-account-hygiene.md` | Poll: *"Which account security step do you still put off?"* |
| **Week 4** | **Safe AI Workflows & Verification** | Fact-checking AI outputs, avoiding malicious AI-suggested packages/links, and building a 5-minute weekly security routine. | Campaign recap visual + downloadable checklist | `week-4-safe-ai-workflow.md` | Prompting readers to save/share the 1-page checklist and comment their #1 habit. |

---

## 5. Measurement & Reach Metrics
Campaign performance will be evaluated across three core dimensions:

1. **Reach & Visibility**
   * **Impressions & Views:** Total post impressions across LinkedIn and repository views/clones on GitHub.
   * **Follower / Profile Growth:** Net new LinkedIn followers and profile visits during the 4-week cycle.
2. **Engagement Quality**
   * **Engagement Rate:** `(Reactions + Comments + Reposts + Poll Votes) / Total Impressions × 100`.
   * **Poll & Quiz Participation:** Total votes and comment responses per weekly interactive prompt.
3. **Action & Retention**
   * **Reposts & Shares:** Number of times educational visuals/checklists are reshared to extend organic reach.
   * **Resource Click-Throughs:** Link clicks from LinkedIn posts to the GitHub resource repository.
